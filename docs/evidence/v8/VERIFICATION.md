New routes: `/rehabilitation` for patient overview, `/rehabilitation?tab=progress` for progress, `/clinician` for the local review overview. `/practice` and `/therapist` remain supported. The six main navigation entries preserve large targets; mobile uses two rows. The caregiver PIN gate is reused for the clinician route.

The weekly target counts saved practices since Monday across all languages and methods. Progress filters apply to the 7/28-day cards, calendar, word comparisons and recent records. Empty/unreviewed measurements do not become zero accuracy; AAC tasks and records using the AAC method remain outside speech/text accuracy, while participation and reported understanding are retained. Time measures are response markers, not acoustic speech onset. The clinician queue is administrative, not a clinical urgency score.

The app remains a local practice and review tool. The active browser databases are not encrypted by Sollu; the PIN and manually entered reviewer names do not establish authenticated clinician access. Imported reports remain separate read-only snapshots. Text matching and descriptive progress do not establish speech recovery or clinical efficacy. Physical-device/audio testing, participant and SLP review, native-language review, independent security review and clinical validation remain outstanding. See [rehabilitation workflow](../../REHABILITATION.md) and [privacy boundaries](../../PRIVACY_AND_VALIDATION.md).

Runtime: existing local web/API servers remain running. The API reports local-only processing and allowCloudAI:false. No stored data in the user’s browser was changed by verification.
